Privacy Policy for AYANET

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled within the AYANET app on iOS.

About the App

AYANET is a simple app designed to help users organize daily tasks, reminders, notes, and personal tracking in an easy and clear way.

Data Collection

AYANET does not collect, sell, or share users’ personal data with third parties.

Data Stored in the App

Users may enter data such as daily tasks, notes, reminders, or personal records inside the app. This data is stored locally on the user’s device and is not sent to our servers.

Sign-In

The app does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its main features.

Notifications

The app may request permission to send notifications. Notifications are used only for purposes such as daily reminders, important app alerts, or app update notifications. Users can disable notifications at any time from their device settings.

Location Data

AYANET does not request, use, track, or store the user’s geographic location. The app does not use location data for advertising, marketing, analytics, payments, financial services, or internet-provider services.

Analytics and Advertising

AYANET does not display in-app advertisements. The app does not sell user data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Technical services may be used only when necessary to operate notifications or improve app stability.

Financial Services

AYANET does not provide banking, electronic payment, money transfer, loan, investment, or financial advisory services. Any financial or expense-related information entered by the user is used only for personal organization.

Internet Services

AYANET does not provide internet service provider features, does not manage internet subscriptions, and does not collect bills or payments related to internet services.

Third-Party Services

The app may use technical third-party services such as Apple notification services or Firebase Cloud Messaging to send reminders, important app alerts, or update notifications. These services are used only to operate notification-related features and are not used to sell user data.

Data Security

We aim to keep locally stored data secure within the user’s device. However, users are responsible for protecting their device, passcode, and screen lock.

User Rights

Users can delete data entered in the app through the available app features or by deleting the app from their device. Users can also disable notifications at any time from the device settings.
